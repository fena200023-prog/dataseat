SHEERM dataset - PV Generation descriptor


There are 13 CSV files corresponding to the generated power given the specificities of the associated solar panel. The CSV file of each house contains of the following variables: 

- Timestamp: Denotes the time at which the data was recorded 

- Cos Solar Incidence (degree): Represents the cosine of the solar incidence angle (cos{theta}), which is the angle between the direction of the sun and the perpendicular to the panel surface. 

- GHI (W/m^2): Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) measures the total amount of shortwave irradiation received from above by a horizontal surface

- PV Power Generation (W): Represents the power generated by photovoltaic (PV) panels in Watts
